Statutory Bye-Laws 2025 Act

The comprehensive, modernized legal framework for Cooperative Housing Societies. Updated for digital compliance, automated accounting, and streamlined community governance.

Chapter I: Mandatory Digital Record Keeping

Under the 2025 Act, societies with more than 20 members are required to maintain digital, immutable records of all financial transactions, statutory registers (I, J, Share Registers), and meeting minutes.

  • All invoices must be digitally generated with timestamping.
  • Cloud-based backups of statutory registers are mandatory.
  • Resident communication must be logged and archived for 7 years.

Chapter II: Virtual AGM & E-Voting Mandates

To increase transparency and resident participation, the new act legalizes and standardizes Virtual Annual General Meetings (AGMs) and cryptographic e-voting for committee elections.

  • Minimum 14-day digital notice period via SMS/Email/WhatsApp.
  • Video recordings of AGMs must be securely stored.
  • E-voting platforms must ensure OTP-based authentication for residents.

Chapter III: Financials, Sinking Funds & Defaulters

The 2025 Act introduces stricter compliance on Sinking Fund utilization and creates an expedited legal framework for recovering dues from chronic defaulters.

  • Sinking fund contributions are now fixed at a minimum of 0.25% of construction cost per annum.
  • Defaulters exceeding 3 months of non-payment face automatic reporting to the Deputy Registrar.
  • Interest on late payments capped strictly at 21% p.a. (simple interest).
