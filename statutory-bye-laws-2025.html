Maharashtra Cooperative Housing Society

Model Bye-Laws Reference Portal (2025 Edition)

Applicable in Maharashtra Last Updated: Jan 2025 Statutory Legal Document

I. Preliminary

1.
Name of the Society: The name of the society shall be Apex Cooperative Housing Society Ltd., registered under the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960.
2.
Registered Address: The registered address of the Society shall be as specified in the registration certificate. Any change in the registered address shall be notified to the Registering Authority within 30 days.

II. Interpretations

3.
Unless the context otherwise requires, words and expressions defined in the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 and the Rules framed thereunder shall have the same meanings.
(a) "Act" means the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 (MCS Act).
(b) "Bye-laws" means the registered bye-laws of the Society, including amendments.
(c) "Committee" means the Managing Committee or other directing body to whom the management of the affairs of the Society is entrusted.
(d) "Flat" means a separate and self-contained set of premises used for residence, office, showroom, or shop.

III. Area of Operation & IV. Objects

4.
Area of Operation: The area of operation of the Society shall be confined to the specific plot or land area detailed in the property conveyance deed.
5.
Objects: The primary objects of the society shall be:
(a) To obtain conveyance from the Builder/Promoter in respect of the land and building.
(b) To manage, maintain, and administer the property of the society on behalf of its members.
(c) To undertake and provide social, cultural, and recreational activities for members.

VI. Funds, Their Utilisation and Investment

6.
Raising of Funds: The funds of the Society may be raised through entrance fees, issue of shares, loans and subsidies, voluntary donations, contributions for flats, and maintenance charges.
7.
Creation of Funds: The Society shall establish and maintain the following funds:
(a) Reserve Fund: As required by Section 66 of the Act.
(b) Repairs and Maintenance Fund: At the rate fixed by the General Body, subject to a minimum of 0.75% per annum of the construction cost of each flat.
(c) Sinking Fund: At the rate decided by the General Body, subject to a minimum of 0.25% per annum of the construction cost of each flat.

XI. General Body Meetings

8.
Supreme Authority: The supreme authority of the Society shall be vested in the General Body of members.
9.
Annual General Meeting (AGM): Every Society shall, within a period of six months after the close of the financial year, call an Annual General Meeting of its members. Notice of 14 clear days must be given to all members.
10.
Special General Meeting (SGM): A Special General Meeting may be called at any time by the Chairman or by a majority of the Managing Committee, or on a requisition signed by not less than one-fifth of the total number of members.
